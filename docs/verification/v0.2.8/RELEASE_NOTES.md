## 🧟 Grow stronger, wave after wave

**Personal co-op growth, distinctive devil supplies, and a cleaner view of the fight.**

### ✨ Highlights

- **Your kills, your growth** — individual LAN scores, levels and XP; a central wave counter tracks both enemy types. Retry keeps wave-start progress; selected-wave runs receive their earned baseline.
- **Armor & ammo drops** — blue armor overlays green health without changing healing; ammunition crates refill every owned weapon. Both have new, distinct artwork.
- **Four survival curves** — tuned pacing and growth across all difficulties, preserving original enemy health, damage and wave totals.

### 🐛 Fixes & polish

- Reduced explosive-chain allocation and rendering overhead; repaired dropped scheduler slots that could accumulate peer lag.
- Fixed armor-bar scaling and ping/HUD overlap. Simplified the bilingual READMEs and organized engineering documents.
