# 🎮 Quick draws, lasting enchantments

## ✨ Highlights

- Bind your held weapon to **A/Y** by holding the button for 0.8 seconds; tap to switch and hold again to unbind. Small key labels and a progress strip show the action. Bindings survive waves, death, saves and reconnects within the run.
- Select every unlocked weapon even with **zero ammunition**. **B/LT/RT** fire, while A still confirms menus and upgrades.
- Enchantments now have **durability independent of ammunition**, with a slim purple meter. Purple crates refill durability; ordinary ammunition does not. Level growth and the new **Enchant Endurance** upgrade raise its capacity.
- A complete comic-paper redesign brings original character portraits, heavy outlines, large upgrade icons, clear benefits and exact previews to the cards and attribute window.

## 🛡️ Fixes and balance

- Armor crates now **fill armor completely**. Start with 160 health and an empty 40-capacity shield; a separate thin cyan bar shows this lighter survival buffer. Damage beyond armor still reaches health.
- **X** and the displayed keyboard shortcut inspect stats only between waves. Single shows your player; local co-op opens P1/P2 together; LAN players independently inspect only themselves.
- Retire ordinary/Boss chargers and their extra 50% crate roll from modern games. Bosses use native fireball devils, or non-charging zombies when devils are disabled.
- Native devil Boss movement, attack progression and damage increase by 15%, 15% and 10%, with staggered initial attacks and unchanged projectile speed/range.
- Preserve legacy LAN rules and v2 saves through optional capabilities and one-time migrations. Reliable one-shot shortcut commands participate in rollback, input repair and recovery; network freezes cancel pending holds.
