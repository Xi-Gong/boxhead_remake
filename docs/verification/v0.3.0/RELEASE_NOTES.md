# v0.3.0 — Endless survival, new threats and enchanted ammunition

## 🧟 Two new enemy variants

- **Bone Chargers** join the zombie family from wave 8. Their bone shoulder armor and rusted bracers accompany a visible warning, a straight rush and a recovery window. Hits interrupt the charge.
- **Ember Priests** join the devil family from wave 20. A modeled horn crown, molten chest markings and wrist armor distinguish their ground-flame spell from the base devil's fireballs.
- Variants replace existing spawn slots. Active limits and clear warnings keep their skills readable, including at LOW detail.

## 💜 Enchant the ammunition you are holding

- Priests always drop one crate: **30% purple enchantment**, otherwise one equally likely armor, ammunition or medical crate.
- Enchantment grants **35% more damage** to the held weapon's current ammunition. Ordinary bullets penetrate two additional enemies; railguns retain their existing piercing. Explosions have stronger edge damage and respect physical walls.
- Purple ammo text, tracers and blast colors identify the effect. Refills add ordinary rounds; switching weapons, recovery crates and saves preserve the enchanted remainder.
- Chargers have a 50% red-box drop chance, combined with the existing combo guarantee into at most one box per kill.

## ♾️ Keep growing beyond level 125

- Levels continue without a gameplay cap. Health, armor capacity, weapon damage and finite ammunition capacity keep growing after the original weapon table ends.
- Enemy health and damage rise with wave progression. Wave budgets stop at **200 zombies and 10 devils**, including variants.
- Starting-wave selection follows your historical best beyond wave 100. High-wave saves restore directly without replaying every earlier wave.

## 🛠️ Reliable pickups and calmer menus

- Supplies refill the correct personal maxima and preserve higher capacities inherited from weapon recovery crates. Single-weapon red rewards fill only the chosen weapon.
- Confirmed P1/P2 pickup messages use a stable three-row HUD queue, including simultaneous pickups, with smooth movement, pause support and fade-out.
- Main-menu hit areas and conditional LAN actions have clearer spacing across wide and tall windows.

New PvE rules apply to solo, local co-op and compatible LAN peers. Death match and older peers retain their supported rules.

Windows portable: **Boxhead 2Play Rooms-0.3.0-portable.exe**. Saves remain in `%USERPROFILE%\.boxhead`.
Release verification is functional and serialized; performance benchmarks and extreme stress are outside this release's authorized QA scope.
